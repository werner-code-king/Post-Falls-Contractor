About the sourcing & its limits

How this list was built

Five research passes ran web searches per trade using patterns like "best <trade> Post Falls ID", "top rated <trade> Coeur d'Alene ID" and "<trade> Kootenai County Idaho reviews". Each business's rating was labeled "Google" only when a source explicitly attributed it to Google; otherwise the actual platform (HomeAdvisor, Angi, Yelp, Thumbtack, BBB, Experience.com, a directory, or the company's own site) is named instead. Businesses were ranked within each trade by rating, weighted by review count, so a perfect score from one or two reviews ranks below a strong score backed by dozens or hundreds — judgment calls like that are noted on the business card.

Known gaps

  • Most ratings come from HomeAdvisor/Angi aggregator snapshots rather than a single canonical platform — only 8 of 93 listings carry a rating explicitly sourced from Google.
  • 20 of 93 listings have no verifiable public rating at all; they're included because they're real, locally findable businesses, not because they're unrated by omission.
  • Phone numbers and street addresses are frequently missing or unconfirmed — many small North Idaho trades operate from a truck or a PO Box rather than a storefront.
  • No license or insurance status is verified anywhere in this directory. A handful of listings mention a license self-report or an aggregator "approved pro" badge; those are noted as unverified claims, never shown as a badge.
  • Some businesses based just across the state line in Spokane/Spokane Valley, WA are included where they clearly advertise serving Post Falls/Coeur d'Alene.

Working now

Suggested next steps
